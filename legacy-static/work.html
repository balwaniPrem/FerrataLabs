The work

Agents we've shipped.

These are examples, not a catalogue. They show the shape of what an agent can own end to end, and where we draw the line between machine and person. What we build for you is designed around your processes — including the ones you have every reason to keep to yourself.

Sterling

Working capital

Reads receivables and payables from the ERP, narrates the cash position daily, and drafts collection sequences ranked by recovery odds.

  • Daily cash position, written not charted
  • Collections prioritised by recovery odds
  • Disputes held back automatically

Clark

Payables

Extracts vendor invoices from any format and posts the payable straight into SAP or NetSuite, with duplicate detection before anything is released.

  • Any format: PDF, scan, portal or email body
  • Duplicate and near-duplicate detection
  • Three-way match with tolerance rules

Tally

Reconciliation

Balances sub-ledgers to the GL line by line and hands back a clean exception list instead of a spreadsheet nobody wants to open.

  • Runs continuously, not at month-end
  • Variance commentary drafted for review
  • Accrual proposals with support attached

Chandler

Procurement

Runs RFQ to PO with multi-vendor negotiation across email, SMS and voice, then holds suppliers to the pricing schedule they agreed to.

  • Multi-vendor RFQ across channels
  • Contract terms extracted and monitored
  • Price variance flagged against schedule

Swift

Order management

Turns inbound customer purchase order PDFs into clean sales orders in minutes rather than days, flagging only what genuinely needs a decision.

  • PO document to sales order in minutes
  • Pricing and terms validated on intake
  • Only true exceptions routed to a person

Quill

Quotes & CRM

Reads every inbound enquiry, prices it against your rules, and drafts the quote in your CRM so a rep is editing rather than starting cold.

  • Every enquiry read, none dropped
  • Priced against your own rule set
  • Drafted in the CRM, ready to edit

Your agents are built around your organization.

The way you price, route, approve and escalate is years of accumulated judgement, and it's a real advantage. It belongs to you and it stays with you — we're not asking you to hand over your playbook or fit yourself into someone else's template. We start from how your business already works, encode that logic into agents built for your environment, and leave you with an operation that runs faster with exactly the things that make you good at it still intact.

What doesn't change: every agent stops at an approval gate until it has earned its way out of one. Nothing releases cash, commits spend or touches a customer without a person saying so.

The difference is execution.

Plenty of tools will now summarise a report or draft an email about a problem. An agent goes and resolves it. Receivables makes the clearest illustration because every business runs it — both columns end in the same place, with a person releasing the money.

Receivables → cash · assisted

  • Pull the aging report out of SAP by hand
  • Guess which accounts to chase first
  • Chase across scattered email threads
  • Reconcile remittances line by line
  • See the true cash position at month-end

Receivables → cash · agent-run

  • Aging and payables read live from the ERP
  • Cash position narrated every morning
  • Collection sequences drafted, prioritised by risk
  • Remittance exceptions listed, not hunted
  • Human approves and releases
